Privacy Policy

Updated and effective: 20 November 2026 · Android app

App: LocalConvert
Developer: AlexRacing98

Offline by design.
Your conversions happen on your device. No account, no file uploads, no tracking.

1. App and developer

LocalConvert is developed and provided by AlexRacing98. This policy covers the LocalConvert Android app, including HUAWEI AppGallery distribution (package name: app.localconvert). It also explains how the public policy website is hosted. For privacy questions, contact zhivkovalexander98@gmail.com.

2. Your files stay on your device

LocalConvert converts and processes files on your device. You do not need a LocalConvert account. The app does not upload your files, text, images, QR contents, or generated passwords to us or to a conversion service. The Android app does not request Internet permission and does not require Google Play Services.

We do not use advertising, tracking, analytics, or automatic crash-reporting services in the app. We do not collect device identifiers, your contacts, or your location. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

3. Information used locally

The app uses only the information needed for the tools and options you choose:
  • Files and text: selected images, documents, folders, and text are processed locally to perform conversions, editing, file organization, scanning, and other requested operations. Some operations use temporary files in the app cache. Text drafts may be saved in private app storage when Android saves the screen state, so work can be restored.
  • Optional name and preferences: the name you enter for the welcome message and your theme preference are saved locally. A name is not required and is not sent to us.
  • Recommendations: the app saves the most recent 128 opened tool IDs locally to recommend tools. This record does not contain file contents, passwords, or QR contents.
  • Recent files: history stores up to 50 result entries, including the file name, location reference, file type, size, tool, and creation time, so you can reopen or share your results.
  • Privacy notice: the policy version you acknowledged is stored locally to avoid repeating the same first-launch notice. Opening the policy does not send an acceptance record to us or grant permissions.
  • Currency tools: calculations use bundled, manually entered, or imported exchange rates. The app does not fetch rates in the background.

4. Permissions, camera, and clipboard

Camera: LocalConvert asks for camera permission when you choose camera QR scanning. Camera frames are processed on your device and are not saved or transmitted by the scanner. You can deny or revoke camera permission in Android Settings; other tools remain available.

Files and folders: Android's file picker lets you choose the files or folders the app can access. Folder tools inspect the folder you select to perform the requested operation. Access to selected saved results may be retained so history can reopen them.

Clipboard: Paste and Copy actions read or write the system clipboard when you choose them. Clipboard contents may also be handled by your operating system or clipboard services according to their settings.

Image files can already contain metadata, such as location tags. LocalConvert does not request your device's location. Use Remove metadata when you want to create an image copy without copied personal metadata; information visible in the picture is not removed.

5. Sharing, external apps, and support

Files leave LocalConvert only through actions you choose, such as sharing, opening a result in another app, or saving to a cloud-backed location. Those apps and storage providers may upload or synchronize files under their own policies and settings.

Report a bug opens an editable draft in your mail app addressed to zhivkovalexander98@gmail.com. The draft includes the app version and prompts for a description. Nothing is sent until you send the email. If you contact us, we receive your email address and whatever message or attachments you choose to send. We use this information to respond, investigate the issue, and handle your request. Email providers process the message to deliver and store it.

Please avoid sending private documents, passwords, or other sensitive content unless necessary for your request. Public GitHub issues are visible to others.

Opening external links, such as the currency-rate source or GitHub, uses your browser and the destination's privacy practices. HUAWEI AppGallery separately handles downloads and store-related information under its own privacy policy.

Bundled libraries: PDFBox, ZXing, AndroidX ExifInterface, TIFF/AVIF/JPEG XL decoders, Kotlin and cryptographic libraries support local document, image and QR operations. They are not advertising or analytics SDKs and are not used to send personal information to their authors.

6. Retention and your choices

Your files remain in the locations you choose until you delete or move them. Removing a history entry removes the reference, not the saved file. Older entries may leave the 50-entry history while their files remain.
  • Edit or clear your optional name in Settings.
  • Use Reset recommendations in Settings to clear the local recommendation record.
  • Remove individual entries from History.
  • Use Android Settings to clear LocalConvert's cache or app data, revoke camera permission, or uninstall the app. Clearing app data removes local settings, history, text drafts, recommendations and the privacy-notice acknowledgement; exported files outside the app remain until you delete them separately.

The app disables Android automatic backup of its private app data. Files you export may still be backed up by a destination or device service you use.

We cannot remotely access or erase files stored only on your device. For information you voluntarily send by email, you can request access, correction, or deletion using the contact address below. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to handle the request and any applicable legal obligations. Depending on your location, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, request portability, or complain to your local data-protection authority.

7. Security and children

Local processing reduces the need to transfer your files. Your device's security and the apps or destinations you choose also affect their protection; no storage or transmission method is guaranteed to be completely secure. Base64 encoding and file checksums are not file encryption.

The app does not require age information or knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. If you believe a child has sent us personal information through support, contact us so we can address it.

8. This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no advertising, analytics scripts, tracking pixels, or third-party fonts added by us. GitHub states that it logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when a GitHub Pages site is visited. This website hosting is separate from LocalConvert's offline file processing. See GitHub Pages documentation and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when LocalConvert functionality or privacy practices change. The updated and effective dates identify the current policy. A changed notice version will be shown again before you use the tools. You can reopen the full policy at any time in Settings → Privacy → Read Privacy Policy; both languages are bundled for offline reading.
